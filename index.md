ConfPulse shows a curated list of developer conferences and their details. This policy
explains what the app does with your data.

The short version: **ConfPulse has no accounts, collects nothing about you, and sends us
nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no server we operate.**

---

## 1. What ConfPulse collects about you

**Nothing.**

There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. The app does not ask for or receive
your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar, location, or advertising
identifier. It contains no analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK.

We do not receive any data from the app, because there is no server of ours for it to send
data to.

---

## 2. What ConfPulse stores on your device

| Data | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Cached conference list** | A copy of the conference list the app last downloaded, saved to the app's private cache so it opens instantly and still works offline. |

That cache contains only the public conference information the app displays — names, dates,
locations, descriptions. It holds nothing about you. Deleting the app removes it.

---

## 3. Network connections the app makes

ConfPulse makes network requests to exactly two kinds of destination, and only to display
content:

- **GitHub** (`api.github.com`) — to download the conference list the app displays. This is
  a read-only request for a single data file. As with any internet request, GitHub receives
  your device's IP address and standard request metadata in order to respond. GitHub's
  handling of that is governed by the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
  We do not send GitHub anything about you, and we do not receive anything back about you.
- **Venue images** — if a conference entry includes a venue image, your device fetches that
  image from wherever it is hosted, and that host likewise sees your IP address as a normal
  consequence of serving the request.

Tapping a conference's **Visit** link opens that conference's own website in your browser.
Once you leave the app, that site's own privacy policy applies; we have no involvement in
what it does.

---

## 4. Tracking

ConfPulse does **not** track you. It does not collect data for advertising, does not build a
profile of you, and does not share, sell, or disclose any personal information — because it
does not collect any in the first place. No App Tracking Transparency prompt appears because
the app has nothing to ask permission for.

---

## 5. Children

ConfPulse is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone,
including children under 13.

---

## 6. Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, export, or
delete on request. Everything the app stores is local to your device and removed when you
delete the app.
